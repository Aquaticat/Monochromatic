## Overview

Morph Apply is a job application tracker redesign completed as a team project
(Team CTRL\_Z) at Algonquin College in 2025.
The goal was to reimagine how job seekers organize, track, and manage
their applications through a more intuitive and visually clear interface.

## My role and contributions

I **led the design effort** for this team project, responsible for:

- Creating the **branding style guide** -- defining the visual identity,
  colour palette, typography, and component styles
- Designing **wireframes** at multiple fidelity levels
- Building **interactive prototypes** in Figma for user testing and stakeholder review
- Creating **presentation materials** communicating design rationale
  and user research findings to stakeholders

## Process

The project followed a structured UX process:

1. **Research** -- Competitive analysis of existing job trackers,
   identifying pain points in application management workflows
2. **Branding** -- Developed a cohesive visual identity that conveys
   professionalism and clarity
3. **Wireframing** -- Iterated from low-fidelity sketches to high-fidelity layouts
4. **Prototyping** -- Interactive Figma prototypes for key user flows
5. **Presentation** -- Pitched the design to stakeholders with supporting research
