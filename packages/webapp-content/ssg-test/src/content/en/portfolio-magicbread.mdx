## Overview

MagicBread.ca is a reasonably complete website design for a fictional
independent bakery, created as an academic project at Algonquin College in 2025.
The project covers the full design thinking process from naming and brand strategy
through competitive analysis to final visual design.

## My role and contributions

I was responsible for the **complete design process**:

- **Brand strategy** -- Naming, values definition, and positioning
- **Competitive analysis** -- Researching existing bakeries to identify
  market gaps and opportunities
- **Persona development** -- Creating customer profiles to guide design decisions
- **Visual design** -- Complete website design in Figma

## Design thinking process

### Naming and brand values

The name "MagicBread" was chosen deliberately:
"Magic" symbolizes creativity that transforms "Bread" --
a common, affordable ingredient -- into something that provides happiness.
The `.ca` domain signals a Canadian, locally-focused business.

Core values identified:
- Serve the general public at reasonable prices
- Cook creative, original pastries
- Be a "little but reassuring flash of warmth" for customers

### Competitive analysis

Researched existing bakeries to identify what works and what falls short:

- Large chains lose the down-to-earth feel when they scale
- Highly rated bakeries often suffer from unreasonable pricing
- Many bakeries underinvest in photography and online presence,
  creating barriers with potential customers

### Design decisions

Based on research, the design emphasizes:

1. Warm, inviting interior imagery through large glass panes and natural light
2. Creative pastry presentation that communicates the "magical" brand
3. Professional photography that reduces barriers with potential customers
4. Down-to-earth pricing and single-location focus
