Marketing taglines from tech companies are bizarrely interchangeable.
 Strip the logo and the name,
 leave just the line,
 and "AI for problem solvers,
" "the cloud that works for you,
" and "the new way to cloud" could each belong to half a dozen vendors.
 The lines below are real:
 a mix from frontier AI labs,
 hyperscalers,
 bare-metal hosts,
 retail giants,
 and edge networks.
 Pick which company you think each one belongs to.
 The question order is reshuffled on every page load,
 so you cannot memorise the sequence;
 the explanation reveals as soon as you pick,
 and you can change your guess at any time.
