## Comparison boundaries

### Visibility

cue before activation.

## Ranked investigation paths

### A. Build the separated comparison

Pros:
addresses unresolved placement and visibility without treating source
retention as a user preference.

Cons:
requires meaningful built alternatives,
verification and the genuine player baseline before incumbent claims.
No superior alternative is established yet.

### B. Preserve the current fixture and defer selection

Pros:
tested distinguishing suffixes stay visible;
no unverified alternative is silently adopted.

Cons:
the presentation preference stays open and no alternative is compared.
This is continuation of the fixture,
not a permanent always-visible policy.

### C. Copy the ordinary-row replica into Search

Pros:
the replica demonstrates a one-line treatment.

Cons:
its tested long-name rows hide the distinguishing suffixes;
it is not the live player and omits Search context and decoration.
It is not a usable comparison option without a visible identifying cue.

Ranking:
A > B > C.
A ranks ahead of B because it can resolve the genuine remaining tradeoff;
B ranks ahead of C because deferral preserves the tested distinctions
rather than adopting a treatment that hides them.
This ranks investigation paths,
not selected extension visibility or placement.
