## Fitting study responsibilities

Freeze the fresh corpus and references before querying.
Keep sibling variants together and separate fit,
selection/calibration,
and locked-test partitions.
No earlier model output becomes a label.
Already queried development examples remain diagnostics,
not independent validation.
Keep all original reserved scenarios unopened.

Use code-owned parser or structured-input projection for operation facts.
Keep complete current `AGENTS.md` in every hypothesis document,
with freshness checks before releasing results and `truncation: false`.
Keep each axiom's support/complement pair separate;
absence of permission is not prohibition.
No raw-score normalization is a probability adapter.

Inspect and test the first-party numerical solver before fitting.
Fit preprocessing and coefficients on the fit partition only.
Use validation evidence to diagnose calibration and abstention;
lock the candidate before the single test-set evaluation.
No production cutoff or automatically validated flag is selected.
Per-role and per-operation reporting must expose failures that pooled metrics can hide.

A completed fit would yield candidate parameters and evaluation evidence,
not production readiness.
Synthetic samples,
limited support,
preview-model stability,
semantic ambiguity,
serving identity,
and real human-authority collection remain evidence limits.
A failed or inconclusive phase is retained,
not rescued by silently changing labels,
templates,
or the locked test.
