      The reasoning behind the result

      Use the existing judgments as the scale

      This is a personal quality philosophy. The reference ratings and their reasoning are the starting point for new assessments.

      1. The defining obligation comes first. A confirmed core failure, trust inversion, wrong-output failure, coercive ecosystem failure, or purpose-defeating privileged machinery forces 0.
      2. Consider defects together. Separate independent causes from repeated symptoms. Compare the software with their combined effects in view. There is no automatic deduction for the number of bullets you write.
      3. Comparisons narrow the possible bands. Higher than a 6 permits 7 through 10. Lower than an 8 permits 0 through 7. Together they identify 7, provided the endpoint checks agree.
      4. 10 has additional requirements. No meaningful major redesign remains, the primary contract is exceptional, governance has no major defect, and the scope is settled. A failed condition excludes 10. An unanswered condition leaves it unconfirmed.
      5. Uncertainty stays visible. A range contains every band compatible with your answers. It is not a probability or confidence interval. Contradictions stay visible until you revise the conflicting answers.

      What the page can decide

      It checks the consequences of your judgments. It does not read the notes as evidence, measure software, or infer quality from a feature count. The next suggested reference is selected to divide the remaining bands; you can always choose a familiar one instead.

      Why there is no weighted average

      A strength in one area cannot automatically cancel a decisive problem elsewhere. The new workflow uses the approved whole-product judgments directly, avoiding invented criterion weights and decimal precision.
