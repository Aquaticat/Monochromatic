What changed and what stayed fixed

  1. Direct target evidence: the installed SettingsGoogle.apk exposes exactly 85%, 100%, 115%, 130%, 150%, 180%, and 200%. The earlier 50% and 75% shell-injected states are excluded.
  2. Dynamic mode label: the third option names the active subdirectory. Its one-row content width is capped by Shuffle Camellia; longer names middle-ellipsize the subdirectory only while accessibility retains the full name.
  3. Adaptive row count: on this measured pane, complete labels try one connected row with variable-width content-sized cells, then a connected 2×2 block, then four connected vertical rows. No state scrolls horizontally.
  4. Accepted 3B: every capture retains the T3 current row, O2 tonal Open, centered transport, 24% seek position, outlined Previous and Next, and filled Pause.
  5. Native evidence: every image is an opaque 2076 × 2152px Compose capture of the 852 × 883dp unfolded Pixel 9 Pro Fold with Android system bars and live insets.

Observed wrapping

85% uses one content-sized row. 100%, 115%, and 130% use 2×2. 150%, 180%, and 200% use four rows.

Padding behavior

Every segment keeps at least 12dp horizontal content padding. The control adds rows rather than reducing that floor. Targets remain at least 48dp.

Assistive technology boundary

Native semantics expose four radio options and the current-track description. Production assistive technology must still verify one mutually exclusive group announcement.

Priority: one row > two rows > four rows. Content-sized cells let Camellia remain on one row at 85% without crossing the 12dp padding floor. Two rows ranks above four because it preserves deck space; four rows is the fallback that keeps every label complete. These are states of one adaptive design, not competing styles.
