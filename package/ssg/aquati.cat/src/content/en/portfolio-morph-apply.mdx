## Overview

Morph Apply is an AI tool redesign completed as a team project
(Team CTRL\_Z) at Algonquin College in 2025.

## My role and contributions

I **led the design effort** for this team project,
 responsible for:

- Creating the **branding style guide**:
   defining the visual identity,
  colour palette,
   typography,
   and component styles
- Designing **wireframes** at multiple fidelity levels
- Building **interactive prototypes** in Figma for user testing and stakeholder review
- Creating **presentation materials** communicating design rationale
  and user research findings to stakeholders

## Process

The project followed a structured UX process:

1. **Research**:
    Competitive analysis of existing AI tools,
   identifying pain points in application management workflows
2. **Branding**:
    Developed a cohesive visual identity that conveys
   professionalism and clarity
3. **Wireframing**:
    Iterated from low-fidelity sketches to high-fidelity layouts
4. **Prototyping**:
    Interactive Figma prototypes for key user flows
5. **Presentation**:
    Pitched the design to stakeholders with supporting research
